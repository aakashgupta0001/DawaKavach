DawaCheck

Nobody should take the same salt twice without knowing it.

One salt is sold under many brand names. A fever tablet and a cold tablet can both be paracetamol, and no single doctor sees the whole list. DawaCheck checks the whole list.

  • 1Scan the bill. OCR reads a photo of a pharmacy bill or medicine box and matches each line to an Indian brand.
  • 2Check the whole list. Repeated salts, known interactions from the dataset, and AI-predicted possible risks — all checked on salts, not brand names.
  • 3Understand it. Every alert in plain Hindi or English, with questions to ask the doctor and cheaper same-salt generics.

DawaCheck never tells anyone to start, stop or change a medicine. Every alert ends with “confirm with your doctor or pharmacist.”
